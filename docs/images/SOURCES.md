# Screenshot sources

These images show the live Margin app using public-domain agency text. The article text was trimmed to narration paragraphs; headings, navigation, captions, and unrelated credits were omitted. No source photographs, agency logos, or third-party illustrations are reproduced. NASA and NOAA do not endorse Margin.

- **Moonlight** — Caela Barry, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. [Article](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moonlight/) · [NASA usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Shown in the editor, generation progress, and player.
- **How Do Coral Reefs Form?** — NOAA National Ocean Service. [Article](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_corals/coral04_reefs.html).
- **Cool Autumn Weather Reveals Nature’s True Hues** — NOAA. [Article](https://www.noaa.gov/stories/cool-autumn-weather-reveals-nature-s-true-hues).

[NOAA’s reuse policy](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/about/faq.html) identifies its articles as public domain unless otherwise noted and requests source credit.

The screenshots were captured at 2× rendering scale from an isolated instance of the unmodified app, using a copy of the demonstration library. They show real app states, including a running generation and its estimated remaining time, and were cropped and saved as PNG. The Margin wordmark reproduces the app’s header as vector paths; a large PNG export is also provided.
